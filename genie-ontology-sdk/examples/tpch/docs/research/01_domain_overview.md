# 01 Domain Overview — tpch_supply_chain

## Summary
TPC-H is a decision-support benchmark whose business queries model a **wholesale supplier** that manages, sells or distributes products worldwide; it is not meant to represent any single business segment ([TPC-H Specification v3.0.1](https://www.tpc.org/TPC_Documents_Current_Versions/pdf/TPC-H_v3.0.1.pdf)). The schema has eight tables: PART, SUPPLIER, PARTSUPP, CUSTOMER, ORDERS, LINEITEM, NATION and REGION ([TPC-H Specification v3.0.1](https://www.tpc.org/TPC_Documents_Current_Versions/pdf/TPC-H_v3.0.1.pdf)). The Databricks `samples.tpch` schema contains data from the TPC-H benchmark ([Sample datasets | Databricks on AWS](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/discover/databricks-datasets)). In business terms: customers place orders of line items for parts supplied by suppliers, across nations and regions.

## Findings

### Scope and current version
* The TPC lists TPC-H 3.0.1 as the current specification ([TPC Current Specs](https://tpc.org/TPC_Documents_Current_Versions/current_specifications5.asp)).
* The benchmark is "comprised of a set of business queries" and its modeled environment supports 7x24 query and refresh operations ([TPC-H Specification v3.0.1](https://www.tpc.org/TPC_Documents_Current_Versions/pdf/TPC-H_v3.0.1.pdf)).

### Core entities and relationships
The spec's schema figure shows these relationships ([TPC-H Specification v3.0.1](https://www.tpc.org/TPC_Documents_Current_Versions/pdf/TPC-H_v3.0.1.pdf)):
* PART to PARTSUPP and SUPPLIER to PARTSUPP (PARTSUPP is the part-supplier pairing).
* CUSTOMER to ORDERS and ORDERS to LINEITEM.
* NATION to CUSTOMER and NATION to SUPPLIER; REGION to NATION.

### Notable coded attributes
* `c_mktsegment` (CUSTOMER), `o_orderstatus` and `o_orderpriority` (ORDERS), `l_returnflag` and `l_linestatus` (LINEITEM) are named in the spec schema ([TPC-H Specification v3.0.1](https://www.tpc.org/TPC_Documents_Current_Versions/pdf/TPC-H_v3.0.1.pdf)).
* Market segments used in query substitution: AUTOMOBILE, BUILDING, FURNITURE, MACHINERY, HOUSEHOLD ([TPC-H Specification v2.17.3](https://www.tpc.org/tpc_documents_current_versions/pdf/tpc-h_v2.17.3.pdf)).
* Order priorities: 1-URGENT, 2-HIGH, 3-MEDIUM, 4-NOT SPECIFIED, 5-LOW ([TPC-H Specification v2.17.3](https://www.tpc.org/tpc_documents_current_versions/pdf/tpc-h_v2.17.3.pdf)).
* `l_extendedprice` is defined in data generation as `l_quantity * p_retailprice` ([TPC-H Specification v2.17.3](https://www.tpc.org/tpc_documents_current_versions/pdf/tpc-h_v2.17.3.pdf)).
* `ps_supplycost` is the supplier's cost for a part ([TPC-R Specification v2.1.0, 1.2 Database Entities](https://www.tpc.org/TPC_Documents_Current_Versions/pdf/tpc-r_v2.1.0.pdf)).

### Stakeholders (as implied by the benchmark queries)
* Sales and pricing (Q1 pricing summary, Q6 forecasting revenue change, Q8 national market share), fulfillment and logistics (Q12 shipping modes and order priority), returns (Q10 returned item reporting), and procurement / inventory (Q11 important stock identification, Q9 product type profit) ([TPC-H Specification v2.18.0](https://www.tpc.org/tpc_documents_current_versions/pdf/tpc-h_v2.18.0.pdf)).

### Standard data model
* The data is generated by the TPC-provided DBGen package, which must be used to populate the database ([TPC-H Specification v2.17.3](https://www.tpc.org/tpc_documents_current_versions/pdf/tpc-h_v2.17.3.pdf); [tpch-dbgen README](https://github.com/databricks/tpch-dbgen/blob/master/README)).

## Relevance to the source tables
| Finding | Table / column | How it is used (measure, dimension, filter, page) |
|---|---|---|
| Customer entity, market segment | `samples.tpch.customer` / `c_mktsegment`, `c_nationkey` | dimension, page (Customer, Market Segment) |
| Order entity, status and priority | `samples.tpch.orders` / `o_orderstatus`, `o_orderpriority`, `o_orderdate`, `o_totalprice` | dimension, filter, measure (order volume) |
| Line item grain, pricing and dates | `samples.tpch.lineitem` / `l_extendedprice`, `l_discount`, `l_tax`, `l_shipdate`, `l_commitdate`, `l_receiptdate`, `l_returnflag`, `l_shipmode` | measures (revenue, on-time, returns), dimensions |
| Part catalog | `samples.tpch.part` / `p_type`, `p_brand`, `p_retailprice` | dimension, page (Part) |
| Part-supplier pairing, cost | `samples.tpch.partsupp` / `ps_supplycost`, `ps_availqty` | measures (supply cost, inventory value) |
| Supplier entity | `samples.tpch.supplier` / `s_nationkey`, `s_acctbal` | dimension, page (Supplier) |
| Geography | `samples.tpch.nation`, `samples.tpch.region` / `n_name`, `r_name` | dimensions (market share by nation / region) |
