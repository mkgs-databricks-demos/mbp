# 01 Domain Overview — bakehouse_retail_sales

## Summary
The domain is retail point-of-sale (POS) sales for a franchised bakery chain. Customers buy baked goods at franchise stores, and each franchise sources ingredients from one supplier. The source data is a synthetic sample that [simulates a bakery franchise business](https://www.selectdataset.com/dataset/8ce7ecbe16c0a62866e1786c900b085e). It follows a star-schema shape: one transaction fact table plus customer, franchise and supplier dimension tables ([Kimball: Fact Tables and Dimension Tables](https://www.kimballgroup.com/2003/01/fact-tables-and-dimension-tables/)). The core analytic questions are revenue, units, basket and payment mix by product, franchise, geography and time, plus how well customers and suppliers are covered.

## Findings

### Franchise entities
* **Franchise.** Under the FTC Franchise Rule, a relationship is a franchise when the franchisee operates under the franchisor's trademark, the franchisor exerts significant control or gives significant assistance, and the franchisee makes a required payment ([FTC Franchise Rule Compliance Guide](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/plain-language/bus70-franchise-rule-compliance-guide.pdf)).
* **Franchisee / franchisor.** The franchisee is the person or company granted the right to do business under the franchisor's trademark or trade name. The franchisor grants that licence and supports the brand ([IFA: What is a Franchise](https://www.franchise.org/franchising-overview/what-is-a-franchise/)).
* **Approved supplier.** Franchise systems often designate approved suppliers that franchisees must buy from ([Franchising.com: Supplier Contracts and System Health](https://www.franchising.com/amp/articles/seizing_an_opportunity_supplier_contracts_and_system_health.html)). An approved supplier list is a register of suppliers that have been vetted and authorised for procurement ([FoodDocs: Approved Suppliers List](https://www.fooddocs.com/food-safety-templates/approved-supplier-list)).

### Retail POS entities
* **POS transaction.** A transaction generated by a store cash register or workstation ([Oracle Retail glossary](https://download.oracle.com/oll/tutorials/OCDMTutorial/html/start_here/glossary.htm)).
* **Product / SKU.** A stock-keeping unit uniquely identifies an item ([NRF / Vend Inventory Management Guide](https://cdn.nrf.com/sites/default/files/2018-10/Vend_Inventory_Management_Guide.pdf)).
* **Unit price.** The unit retail price in the selling unit of measure for an item and location ([Oracle Retail Merchandising glossary](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E79623_01/rms/pdf/192000/html/user_guide/glossary.htm)).
* **Basket / ticket.** Basket analysis covers product mix, number of baskets and basket value. A ticket is the record of the items sold with their prices ([Oracle Retail Category Management glossary](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E75765_01/catman/pdf/1411/html/ug/glossary.htm)).
* **Tender type / payment method.** The form of payment used for a sale, for example cash, credit card or gift card ([Oracle Retail Insights: Dimensions and Attributes](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E69213_01/pdf/1800025/html/user_guide/dimensions_attributes.htm)).

### Standard data model
* **Fact table and grain.** Facts are the numeric measurements of a business process event. Declaring the grain fixes exactly what one fact row represents ([Kimball: Facts for Measurements](https://www.kimballgroup.com/data-warehouse-business-intelligence-resources/kimball-techniques/dimensional-modeling-techniques/facts-for-measurement/); [Kimball: Grain](https://www.kimballgroup.com/data-warehouse-business-intelligence-resources/kimball-techniques/dimensional-modeling-techniques/grain/)).
* **Dimension table.** Dimension tables give the who, what, where, when, why and how context used for filtering and grouping ([Kimball: Dimensions for Descriptive Context](https://www.kimballgroup.com/data-warehouse-business-intelligence-resources/kimball-techniques/dimensional-modeling-techniques/dimensions-for-context/); [Microsoft Learn: Star schema](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/guidance/star-schema)).
* **Conformed dimension.** A dimension shared by several fact tables, with the same column names and contents, so their facts can be combined in one report ([Kimball: Conformed Dimensions](https://www.kimballgroup.com/data-warehouse-business-intelligence-resources/kimball-techniques/dimensional-modeling-techniques/conformed-dimension/)).

### Stakeholders
* The franchisor's sales, operations and supply-chain teams track system performance and approved-supplier compliance ([Franchising.com](https://www.franchising.com/amp/articles/seizing_an_opportunity_supplier_contracts_and_system_health.html)).
* Franchisees and prospective franchisees look at unit-level sales figures. In the US, the franchisor discloses these in FDD Item 19 ([FTC: A Consumer's Guide to Buying a Franchise](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumers-guide-buying-franchise)).

## Relevance to the source tables
| Finding | Table / column | How it is used (measure, dimension, filter, page) |
|---|---|---|
| POS transaction (fact) | `samples.bakehouse.sales_transactions` (`transactionID`, `dateTime`) | Fact grain; transaction count; time dimension |
| Product / SKU | `sales_transactions.product` | Dimension (product mix) |
| Unit price, quantity, total | `sales_transactions.unitPrice`, `quantity`, `totalPrice` | Measures: revenue, units, average unit price |
| Tender type | `sales_transactions.paymentMethod` | Dimension: payment mix (values deferred to EDA) |
| Customer | `samples.bakehouse.sales_customers` (`customerID`, `city`, `state`, `country`, `continent`, `gender`) | Dimension; customer KPIs; contains personal data (see 03) |
| Franchise / store | `samples.bakehouse.sales_franchises` (`franchiseID`, `name`, `city`, `district`, `country`, `size`, `supplierID`) | Conformed dimension for sales and supply; store geography |
| Approved supplier | `samples.bakehouse.sales_suppliers` (`supplierID`, `ingredient`, `approved`, `continent`, `size`) | Supplier dimension; supplier coverage KPIs |

## Open questions
* Grain of `sales_transactions`: is it one row per transaction or one row per transaction line? Each row has a single `product`. Session 04 (EDA) must confirm whether `transactionID` is unique.
* Does `totalPrice` equal `quantity * unitPrice`, and are prices in one currency? The franchises span several countries.
* Codes in `paymentMethod`, franchise and supplier `size`, and supplier `approved` are deferred to EDA.
* `sales_franchises.supplierID` implies one supplier per franchise. Session 04 must verify the join cardinality.
