## Definition

Sales after deductions such as returns, allowances and discounts; retailers report comparable store sales on net sales ([SEC 10-K filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1051251/000119312506087300/d10k.htm)).

## Business context

Net sales is the standard revenue line in retail financial reporting.
